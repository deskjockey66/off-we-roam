These are suggested return-visit plans built around **Namba in Osaka** and **Asakusa in Tokyo**. They are accommodation areas, not a claim about the exact hotels we used. I would choose a room on a quieter street within a manageable walk of the station, then check the route with luggage before booking. Staying close to the busiest signs is less useful if nobody sleeps well.

Namba puts the southern Osaka districts within reach. Asakusa gives the Tokyo stay a neighbourhood to return to, with Sensoji and the river nearby. Neither base makes every attraction a short journey. The point is to choose one place in each city and accept the travel time when heading elsewhere, rather than move hotels to chase every stop.

Twenty-one days lets you stay in each area without turning the holiday into a series of check-ins. I would use nine nights in Namba and eleven in Asakusa, leaving on day twenty-one. There is room for more day trips, but I would still separate the longer ones with local days.

## Days 1–5: Start in Osaka

**Day 1:** Arrive and settle in Namba. Keep food and any evening walk close to the accommodation.

**Day 2:** Browse Namba and Dotonbori. Let a meal take the time it needs instead of scheduling a paid attraction immediately afterwards.

**Day 3:** Choose the castle park, with the museum only if it interests the family. Entry arrangements and prices need their own check.

**Day 4:** Stay local and explore more slowly. Shinsaibashi can fit here if shopping is appealing; otherwise, keep the morning light.

**Day 5:** Take a Nara day trip after checking the route and the walking. Make Nara the destination for the day rather than attach another city on the way back.

## Days 6–9: A wider outing, with space around it

**Day 6:** Have a quieter Osaka day. This is the time for ordinary jobs and returning to a place you enjoyed.

**Day 7:** Choose one Kyoto area for a day trip. The choice depends on your interests, and crossing Kyoto to collect every famous site would defeat the point. Research that day's route separately.

**Day 8:** Pick a family activity in Osaka or keep the day free. If a theme park matters, use this day and arrange admission in advance rather than add it to another full day.

**Day 9:** Revisit somewhere nearby and pack for the transfer. A quiet last Osaka evening is useful before moving the bags.

## Day 10: Take the Shinkansen

Travel from Shin-Osaka to Tokyo or Shinagawa, then onward to Asakusa. Allow for the connections at both ends. This is a transfer day with a train lunch, not a normal sightseeing day interrupted by a short ride.

## Days 11–15: Settle into eastern Tokyo

**Day 11:** Explore Asakusa around Sensoji and the nearby streets.

**Day 12:** Choose Ueno, checking any museum opening day before leaving.

**Day 13:** Keep the morning local. Kappabashi is an option for kitchenware browsing, or take a short river walk if conditions suit.

**Day 14:** Visit Yokohama on a museum operating day. Book the Cup Noodles activity you want and leave time for the waterfront or Chinatown afterwards.

**Day 15:** Have a quieter day back in Tokyo. Keep it movable if the museum's calendar requires a different day.

## Days 16–21: Explore without changing hotels

**Day 16:** Make Shibuya the main area, with any timed activity arranged separately.

**Day 17:** Return to something you liked or spend the day around Asakusa. A repeat visit is a perfectly good use of a longer stay.

**Day 18:** Consider a separate day trip such as Kamakura if you want another outing. Check the route and choose a manageable area there. It is optional, not a requirement for the plan to feel complete.

**Day 19:** Choose one more Tokyo district around the family's interests. Keep the journey sensible rather than crossing the city repeatedly for individual stops.

**Day 20:** Leave a flexible final full day and pack. It is useful space for weather changes, shopping you still want to do or a favourite meal.

**Day 21:** Depart with the airport transfer planned around your actual flight.


## The transfer and the money

Use **Shin-Osaka**, not Osaka Station, for the Tokaido Shinkansen. Book seats together and include the local connection from Namba. At the Tokyo end, choose Tokyo or Shinagawa based on the onward route. Asakusa is a further local journey, so leave room for that before expecting to be checked in.

For a starting allowance, use ¥15,000–16,000 per adult for one Ordinary reserved-seat intercity journey, then obtain the actual fare. That is a rounded budget, not a quoted ticket price. A child needing a reserved seat may need a child ticket; check the category rather than assume the adult fare covers the family. Food, accommodation and local transport are additional.

I would not automatically buy the national Japan Rail Pass for this route. Compare the individual journeys first. Two city bases and one intercity transfer rarely justify a national pass simply because the holiday lasts a week or longer. Our [Shinkansen guide](/stories/shinkansen-osaka-tokyo/) covers booking, luggage and the mountain-facing seats.

For the museum day, two adult admissions and three custom cups currently total ¥2,500 when the child qualifies for free museum entry. That example excludes food and other activities. Check the exact activity booking and the date calendar in our [Yokohama guide](/stories/yokohama-cupnoodles-museum/).

## Keep the practical bookings ahead of the rest

Reserve the important activity first, then move the flexible days around it. Cup Noodles Museum normally closes on Tuesdays, with holiday exceptions. Do not fix a Tuesday museum day from this sequence without checking. Any paid observation deck, special exhibition or theme park should have its own opening and ticket check.

These plans assume arriving in the Osaka area and leaving from the Tokyo area. If your flights require returning to Osaka, add the second train journey and the time it takes. I would not silently squeeze that return into an already full departure day. International flights and accommodation prices depend on your actual dates, so they are not hidden inside a made-up total here.
