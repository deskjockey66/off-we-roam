We're a husband and wife from Australia, travelling the world with our daughter. We left our corporate jobs because we wanted a little more space for the things we kept saying we would do one day.

Not a perfect life. A different chapter.

**Off We Roam** is where we keep it: the places that stay with us, the small moments between them and the practical details we wish were easier to find when planning a trip.

You'll find family journals, destination guides and itineraries built around a pace that leaves room to enjoy being there. Our photographs are our own. Our stories belong to the three of us, and the guides are for anyone planning their own version of the journey.

We're starting these pages in the Canadian Rockies—with Moraine Lake, Lake Louise and a ride above the valley. There will be more as we go.

Thanks for being here. We hope something you find makes your next trip feel a little more possible.
